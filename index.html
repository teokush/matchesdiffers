Lean is how far the chosen digit sits from the expected 10%. For Matches the digit is the most frequent one, for Differs it is the least frequent one. Held-out is how much of that lean was still there in the second half of the sample, which the pick never saw. Chance alone usually produces a z near 2.5 across ten digits, so only a z above 3.9 is flagged.
